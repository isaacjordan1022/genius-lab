# Aharon Zev Enrichment Curriculum — v4

Published as `lesson-v4-1` through `lesson-v4-5` and `weekly-test-v4` (`version: 4`). Historical `v3` and `v2` lessons, attempts, and earned tickets remain preserved in `src/lib/store.ts`.

## Core Pillars (Zero Math)

1. **Vocabulary & Word Workshop**: 4 target vocabulary words per day (20 across the week) with definitions, example sentences, and an embedded **Quick Word Lightning Check** mini-game inside `src/components/enrichment-visuals.tsx`.
2. **Short-Chunk Analytical Reading Comprehension**: Concise 3-paragraph passages (~140–160 words) with numbered paragraphs for evidence marking, ElevenLabs ReadAlong word highlighting, and voice-graded open analytical responses.
3. **How Things Work (Leather → Pro Baseball Glove & Baseball Aerodynamics)**:
   - Day 1: Raw Hide to Tanned Leather (`leather-tanning-lab`)
   - Day 2: Full-Grain vs. Split Suede (`hide-layers-explorer`)
   - Day 3: Inside-Out Baseball Glove Blueprint & Impact Lab (`glove-anatomy-lab`)
   - Day 4: Breaking In the Pocket & Fiber Memory (`glove-breakin-simulator`)
   - Day 5: 108 Stitches & Curveball Wind Tunnel (`baseball-seam-windtunnel`)
4. **Torah, Gadol & Halacha**:
   - Day 1: Parshat Noach 3-Deck Teivah Blueprint (`teivah-blueprint-explorer`)
   - Day 2: Rav Yisroel Salanter & The Shoemaker's Candle (`salanter-storybook`)
   - Day 3: Hilchot Shabbos — The 3 Golden Keys of *Borer* (`shabbos-borer-lab`)
   - Day 4: Parshat Noach — The Raven, The Dove & The Olive Leaf (`noach-window-timeline`)
   - Day 5: Hilchot Shabbos — Interactive *Muktzeh* Explorer (`shabbos-muktzeh-explorer`)
